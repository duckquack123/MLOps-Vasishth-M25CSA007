📈 Key Findings

🏗️ Model Architecture

ResNet-18 consistently outperforms ResNet-50 on both datasets. Smaller models generalize better on simple datasets.

⚙️ Optimizer

Adam optimizer achieves higher accuracy than SGD and converges faster with less sensitivity to learning rate.

📊 Learning Rate

lr=0.001 generally produces better results than lr=0.0001 for faster convergence in limited epochs.

⚡ GPU Performance

GPU provides 5-10x speedup over CPU, with larger speedups for bigger models (ResNet-50).
